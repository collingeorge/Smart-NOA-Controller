## Deliberately not changed

- **Code finding 13 (CSP/frame-ancestors as HTTP headers).** These need the hosting server's configuration. A static repository cannot set response headers, and `frame-ancestors` is ignored in a meta tag. The meta CSP is unchanged.
- **Ketorolac rules for "prophylactic before major surgery" and "intraoperative haemostasis" stay as warnings, not locks.** The simulator cannot know whether surgery is major or whether haemostasis is critical. Both show the label wording.
- **Fentanyl elderly dose.** The label gives no reduced figure, so none was invented. The warning asks the user to reduce the dose.
- **Lidocaine WPW** was also made a LOCK, because the S28 label lists it among the contraindications.
- **MAC obese-OSA example.** After the midazolam recalibration it holds "moderate" only briefly before the procedure starts, and is then mostly "minimal". The plan doses are label lower bounds, so this outcome comes from the illustrative model and is not a dosing change.
- **`EXAMPLE_RESULTS.json`.** The Python fixes do not change any example output. The regenerated output is byte-identical.

## Emergency-rescue stress matrix

Owner-approved task: a deterministic matrix asking whether the rescue interventions the engine offers work in its scripted emergencies across extreme fictional patients, and whether the safety rules hold in every run, including abusive dosing. Full results: [`EMERGENCY_STRESS.md`](EMERGENCY_STRESS.md), generated by `node tools/emergency-stress-report.mjs` (≈ 40 s on 4 cores). Driver: `tests/emergency-stress.mjs`. A deterministic subset (≈ 200 runs) is in `npm test`: `tests/emergency-stress-2026-11.test.mjs` (ES0–ES7). Everything below is illustrative model output; nothing was tuned to pass, and every dose comes from `recommend()`.

**Matrix (1,812 runs).** 6 emergencies (every non-sensor complication in `data/scenarios.js`) × 12 fictional patients (age 18 and 90; 40 kg / BMI 14.7 and 200 kg / BMI 65 with OSA; eGFR 10 and 120; ASA 1 and 4; HFrEF + CAD; beta-blocker with baseline HR 52; the elderly CAD/CKD, obese OSA and MH-susceptible examples; hypovolaemia; sepsis) × arms (control, rescue, aggressive, plus IM epinephrine for anaphylaxis) × 2 onset times × 3 seeds = 1,368 runs. Added to these: 108 runs of a "lightened" light-anaesthesia variant and 336 runs that combine an emergency with a fault or hold (MAP, HR or SpO2 sensor fault; latched hold; automatic mode with "Continue with hold latched"). Plans come from the `ga_opioid` template (propofol TIVA fallback when sevoflurane is locked) or `ga_opioid_free` for the hold combinations. Every plan passes the plan-safety check.

**Safety: 0 runs with a violation, 0 exceptions.** Every run is checked on every simulated second and on every administration:
- no NaN or Infinity; NaN appears only on a faulted channel;
- vitals stay within plausible bounds (MAP 20–220, HR 20–250, SpO2 0–100; the physiology clamps at 25–200 / 40–100);
- every accepted dose is within every hard per-dose, per-route and cumulative limit, and is given exactly as ordered (no silent clamping);
- every refusal is logged as BLOCKED and nothing is given (162,689 refusals, almost all in the aggressive arm);
- every accepted dose outside the regimen range or inside the label repeat interval carries a warning;
- running rates stay at or below the label and Python ceilings, and a direct state write is re-validated;
- holds latch one control iteration after MAP < 60, HR < 48 or an unusable reading, and never release without `resume_hold`;
- in advisory runs, vasopressor doses = accepted advisories = "ADVISORY accepted" log lines; the control arm gets no vasopressor;
- in the fault and hold combinations the rescue path (FiO2, fluids, rescue bolus) stays available, and held infusions cannot restart.

**Findings (reported, not fixed; numbers in EMERGENCY_STRESS.md):**
1. At the default plan MAP is already < 65 before any emergency in 7/12 patients.
2. Hemorrhage is not fully reversed in the HFrEF and sepsis patients: 3/6 runs each, worst MAP 36 and 39. The bleeding never stops, fluids offset ≤ 80 %, and the advisory stops at the phenylephrine 200 mcg and ephedrine 50 mg label maxima.
3. Glycopyrrolate 0.1 mg [S8] shortens the self-limiting vagal event only slightly (it uses generic illustrative PK, ke0 0.3/min). The largest effect is with the beta-blocker: 239 → 131 s.
4. Bronchospasm in a ventilated patient on FiO2 0.5 never desaturates (worst SpO2 98 %). It shows as hypercapnia (PaCO2 up to 67 mmHg). Epinephrine 50 mcg IV keeps PaCO2 < 60.
5. Laryngospasm desaturates only the obese patients (FiO2 0.5 through extubation), and with FiO2 1.0 none.
6. The light-anaesthesia stimulus is fully blunted at the default maintenance (worst MAP 103, index 49). MAP never exceeds 120 even when lightened.
7. IV epinephrine works fastest for anaphylaxis, and IM is slower (absorption is perfusion-dependent).
8. Soft limits in the aggressive arm: per run, up to 60.5 mg IM epinephrine, 6.05 mg IV epinephrine and 12.1 mg glycopyrrolate were accepted, all with warnings. No cumulative limit exists for IV epinephrine boluses, glycopyrrolate or crystalloid. HR sits at the 200/min clamp in the aggressive sepsis runs.
